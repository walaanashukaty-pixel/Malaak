# Malaak V5 Intervention Source Registry

This registry documents the evidence sources used to govern the *underlying principles* of Malaak intervention cards. It does not claim that Malaak's exact wording has been clinically validated, and it does not grant permission to copy protected manuals or questionnaires.

## Core sources

- WHO (2026), **Psychological self-help interventions** — https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240120785 — structured low-intensity self-help; licensing requires separate commercial review.
- WHO (2020), **Doing What Matters in Times of Stress** — https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789240003927 — grounding/unhooking/values/self-kindness principles; Malaak uses original wording.
- NICE CG113 — https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg113/chapter/Recommendations — stepped care, CBT-based self-help, outcome monitoring, escalation when impairment/non-response is marked.
- SAMHSA, **Trauma-Informed Approaches and Programs** — https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs — safety, trust, choice, collaboration, empowerment and avoiding re-traumatization.
- McRae & Gross (2020), **Emotion regulation**, PMID 31961170 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31961170/ — process-based emotion-regulation framework.
- Stenzel et al. (2025), **CBT for repetitive negative thinking**, PMID 39916353 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39916353/ — transdiagnostic RNT/rumination/worry evidence.
- Wilson et al. (2023), **Intolerance of uncertainty treatment meta-analysis**, PMID 37271039 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37271039/.
- Näsling et al. (2024), **Psychotherapy on intolerance of uncertainty**, PMID 39036833 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39036833/ — cautions about heterogeneity/active controls.
- Macri & Rogge (2024), **Psychological flexibility mechanisms in ACT**, PMID 38615492 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38615492/.
- Hede et al. (2026), **Assertiveness training RCT meta-analysis** — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41042-026-00297-7.
- Gillath et al. (2022), **Attachment Security Priming: A Meta-Analysis**, PMID 35209765 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35209765/ — used cautiously for attachment-process framing, not fixed labels.
- Rathgeber et al. (2019), **EFT/BCT couple therapy meta-analysis**, PMID 29781200 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29781200/.
- Roddy et al. (2016), **Integrative Behavioral Couple Therapy review**, PMID 27226235 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27226235/.
- DiGiuseppe & Tafrate, **Anger treatment meta-analytic review**, PMID 14992805 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14992805/.
- Han & Kim (2023), **Self-compassion intervention meta-analysis**, PMID 37362192 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37362192/.
- Zhang et al. (2021), **Childhood maltreatment and self-compassion meta-analysis**, PMID 34510982 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34510982/ — association only; never used to reconstruct memories.
- Komischke-Konnerup et al. (2024), **Grief-focused CBT meta-analysis**, PMID 38573714 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38573714/ — supports grief as an intervention target but Malaak does not treat/diagnose prolonged grief disorder.
- Galloway et al. (2021/2022), **CBT for perfectionism meta-analysis**, PMID 34346282 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34346282/ — evidence applies to perfectionism/rigidity, not to the feminine-balance taxonomy itself.

## Governance notes

- `BAL_*` and `FI_*` taxonomy-level cards are tier D coaching constructs. Stronger evidence attached to an underlying skill does not upgrade the taxonomy itself.
- `CHILD_*`, `HEAL_*`, and relationship-repair cards remain draft until a qualified clinical-boundary review is completed for production activation.
- No card copies a protected therapy manual, questionnaire, WHO script, book exercise, or proprietary course.
- Trauma exposure, EMDR, memory recovery, deep imaginal reliving, OCD ERP treatment pathways, forced forgiveness, and manifestation-as-science remain prohibited.
